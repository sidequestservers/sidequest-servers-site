Palworld FAQ

Answers before you start.

What to expect from your SideQuest Palworld server, from crossplay to updates.

Does Palworld have crossplay?

Palworld servers support crossplay across PC, PS5, and Xbox. One server lets everyone play together, so your whole group shares the same world no matter which platform they play on.

Can I use mods on my Palworld server?

No, mod support is not currently offered for our Palworld servers. However, you can fully customize gameplay variables using the Easy-Config tab within the Console Interface.

Do you support Palworld updates and expansions, like the 1.0 release?

Yes. Our Palworld servers stay current with game updates, including the major 1.0 release. When a new version releases, servers update automatically so your world is always running the latest version.
