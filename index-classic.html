FAQ

Questions Palworld players usually ask.

Here are answers to the most common hosting questions. We’ll keep this updated as the game — and SideQuest Servers — evolve.

Where are your servers located?

We’re a US‑based team and start with US regions first. As our community grows, we’ll add more locations based on where people are actually playing.

Can I change my player count later?

Contact us when your group outgrows its current plan. We will confirm the available upgrade options before making any change.

Do I really have full control?

You can manage server settings and scheduled tasks through the panel, so you can tune your Palworld world to your group instead of being stuck with defaults.

What about backups and restarts?

Every plan includes automatic daily backups and scheduled restarts. You can download backups and restore them yourself through the panel.

Can I get a refund or cancel?

Eligible new services may be refunded within 24 hours of the initial purchase. You can cancel before your next renewal date; your server remains active through the paid period.

What happens to my data after cancellation?

Server data is removed 14 days after termination. Download any backups or world files you need before then.

Support & billing

Need help with a plan or payment?

For plan changes, cancellation requests, or billing questions, email support@sidequestservers.com from the address used at checkout. Support is handled in CST; please allow up to 48 hours for a response. Read our service policies for refunds, backups, cancellation, and data deletion.
